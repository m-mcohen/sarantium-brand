# Sarantium voice

Read before writing any brand copy: site copy, marketing collateral, slide text,
OG card lines, case-study prose, outreach.

## Register

**Serious literary magazine meets independent investment research.** Declarative,
specific, compressed.

The brand **does** the thing. It never "helps you" do anything. No abstract verbs,
no buzzwords, no soft framing. Numbers prove the point; the copy does not oversell.

**First person plural** for brand voice. "I" only inside a solo-engagement case
study, where one named person did the work.

## The name

Sarantium is the fictional Byzantium of Guy Gavriel Kay's *Sarantine Mosaic*, whose
protagonist is a mosaicist. The mosaic is the **conceptual spine**, not decoration:
data work is the assembly of disaggregated pieces into a coherent picture. It
governs design, voice, and engineering decisions.

Do not over-explain the reference in customer-facing copy. It earns its place by
structuring the work, not by being narrated.

## Banned words

Never write these:

- *synergy*
- *leverage* (as a verb)
- *transformation*
- *world-class* / *best-in-class*
- *helps you* / *helps companies*
- *fractional CFO* (Sarantium is finance and data infrastructure, not CFO services)

## Banned phrases

**These matter more than the word bans.** They are the new-cliché stack that emerged
*because* of the word bans: a model told to avoid "synergy" and "leverage" reaches
for these instead. Every one of them was written by an assistant trying to sound
on-brand.

- *"we sit between"* (finance and data, X and Y, anywhere)
- *"at the moments their books can't keep up"*
- *"the data existed, the systems existed, but nothing talked to anything else"*
- *"where books and data meet"*
- *"beautifully simple"*, *"quietly powerful"*, or any humble-craftsman adjectival
  stack

If you catch yourself writing one, the sentence is a tell. Rewrite the thought, do
not swap the phrase.

## Punctuation and typography

- **No em-dashes.** Use commas, colons, or a full stop. This is a standing owner
  instruction for new copy. Existing shipped copy contains many; that is history,
  not a licence, and not a cleanup mandate either.
- **No emoji.** Anywhere. Including as icons or bullets.
- **No exclamation points.**
- **No drop caps.** Rejected during brand development.
- **Sentence case** in body and most headlines. Title case is for proper nouns and
  the wordmark.
- **ALL CAPS** with wide tracking (`--ls-eyebrow`) in JetBrains Mono, for eyebrow
  labels only.
- **Wordmark** is Cinzel all-caps at `--ls-wordmark`.

## Italic

Legitimate for exactly three things:

1. **Pull quotes.** Whole sentence italic, no quotation marks. The italic *is* the
   quote signal.
2. **Foreign words.**
3. **Titles of works.**

Never italicize body copy for emphasis. If a sentence needs emphasis, rewrite it.

Note that Cinzel has no italic. Anything italic is Crimson Pro by definition, which
means headlines cannot be italicized at all.

## Colour in copy

No colour emphasis on body copy or headings. The homepage hero inlay chips are the
single sanctioned exception and are locked to that one surface. See `SKILL.md`.

## Case-study and evidence conventions

- Byline: `Engagement Lead: <name>`
- Stat labels in mono caps, e.g. `LABOR SAVINGS DELIVERED`, `MONTH-END CLOSE
  ACCELERATION`
- **Outcome lines name the specific finding, not the vague improvement.** Write
  "$40,000 depreciation error identified," not "material improvements to fixed
  asset accounting."
- **Work and case-study sections show real client engagements only.** Never the
  demo companies. The demos are product surfaces; presenting them as client work
  misrepresents the practice.

## Audience

For PE operating partners and owners of small and mid-sized businesses. Write to a
reader who runs a business and reads financial statements, not to a procurement
form and not to a developer.

## Legal

The public marketing site carries a disclaimer: a homepage fine-print line plus a
full section on `/privacy`. The text is generic **draft** legal language pending a
counsel pass. If counsel adds investment-adviser or securities clauses, both
surfaces must be updated together.
